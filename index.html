SupperTrove keeps your household’s recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists together.

Contact support

Email 4artery_orcas@icloud.com. Include your app version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, session tokens, or other credentials.

TestFlight feedback

In Apple’s TestFlight app, select SupperTrove and choose Send Beta Feedback. A screenshot and the steps that led to the issue help us investigate.

Account and household help

Contact support if you need help with access, invitations, privacy, or deleting your account. Deleting a shared household affects other members; tell us which operation you need help with.
