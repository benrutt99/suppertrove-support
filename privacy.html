SupperTrove Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacyTerms

Effective November 4, 2026. This policy describes how SupperTrove handles information when you use its app and web service.

Information we use

We store account details such as your name, email address, username, and authentication records. We also store the recipes, photos, meal plans, shopping lists, kitchen preferences, and household membership information you provide. Passwords are handled by the authentication service as password hashes, rather than stored as readable passwords.

Household sharing

Recipes, plans, shopping lists, and kitchen preferences belong to the household you select and are available to its authorized members. Do not add information you do not intend to share with those members.

Photos, camera, and notifications

When you choose to capture or select photos, the app uses the requested camera or photo-picker access for that feature. Uploaded photos are stored by the service. Cooking timers can schedule local device notifications after you grant permission. The app uses the device’s biometric authentication system for optional app lock; SupperTrove does not receive or store your biometric templates.

AI and recipe imports

If you use an AI feature, the recipe content, selected photos, instructions, or relevant household kitchen preferences needed for that request may be sent to the configured AI provider for processing. Importing a recipe URL causes the service to retrieve content from that site. These features are optional. We do not sell your personal information or use an advertising SDK in the app.

Service operations

Hosting, database, file-storage, email, and configured AI providers process information needed to deliver the service. Operational and security records can include timestamps, IP addresses, device/user-agent information, and error details. Access is used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve SupperTrove.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and household content while needed to provide the service. You can request account deletion by contacting 4artery_orcas@icloud.com; the app’s account controls also explain the available deletion process. Personal account deletion and shared household deletion are separate. Shared recipes may remain for other household members with author identity removed. Security records and backup copies may persist for a limited operational or required retention period. Contact us for questions about your information and deletion requests.

Your choices

You can edit household content as your role permits, sign out devices, disable optional app lock or notifications, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Device permissions can be managed in iOS Settings.

Contact and updates

Contact 4artery_orcas@icloud.com with privacy questions. We may update this page as the service changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
